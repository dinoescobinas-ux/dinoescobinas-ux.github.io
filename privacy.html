Privacy policy – NTE Sales Mailer

NTE Sales Mailer is used only by its owner, with the single Google account nteupdate@gmail.com.

What it accesses

  • gmail.send: to send the company's daily sales report emails.
  • gmail.metadata: to read the subject, recipient and date of messages in the Sent folder, only to avoid sending the same report twice in one day.

What it does not do

It does not read the body or attachments of any email, does not store Gmail data, and does not sell, share or transfer Gmail data to anyone. Google user data is used only to provide the sending function described above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
